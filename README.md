College Cross-ranking Analysis cross-references five major global university ranking systems — QS World University Rankings, Times Higher Education (THE), ARWU/ShanghaiRanking, CWUR Global 2000, and US News Best Global Universities — to identify US universities that place highly in all five simultaneously, rather than just one or two.

Each school's consensus score is the simple average of its rank across all five systems, using each publisher's current-release figures (no aggregators or approximations). Results are filterable by cutoff (Top 20 / 25 / 30 / 50, with deeper cutoffs available), and schools relying on ARWU's banded rather than exact rankings are flagged explicitly.

Regenerated periodically as new ranking editions are released.
